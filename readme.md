# PalyonDofus — Reverse Engineering Permission

## Authorization

The owner and developer of **Palyondofus** explicitly authorizes reverse engineering of the PalyonDofus software for legitimate purposes, including:

- Security research and vulnerability analysis
- Software interoperability
- Compatibility testing
- Debugging and troubleshooting
- Educational and research purposes
- Analysis of software behavior and protocols
- Decompilation and disassembly
- Inspection of executable files and application resources
- Dynamic and static analysis
- Documentation of internal software behavior

Researchers and users are permitted to inspect, analyze, decompile, disassemble, debug, and study PalyonDofus for these purposes.

## Permitted Techniques

This authorization includes the use of reverse-engineering and debugging tools, including static analyzers, debuggers, decompilers, disassemblers, network-analysis tools, and similar research utilities.

Researchers may create scripts, documentation, proofs of concept, and compatibility implementations based on their analysis.

## Restrictions

This permission does **not** authorize:

- Unauthorized access to third-party systems or accounts
- Theft or disclosure of user credentials or personal data
- Distribution of malware
- Fraudulent activity
- Attacks against PalyonDofus users or infrastructure
- Circumvention of laws or third-party rights

Reverse engineering must be conducted only on software, systems, accounts, and environments for which the researcher has the necessary authorization.

## Security Research

Security vulnerabilities discovered during research may be documented and responsibly reported to the PalyonDofus project.

Good-faith security research performed within the scope of this authorization is explicitly permitted by the PalyonDofus project.

## Scope

This permission applies to official versions of **PalyonDofus** distributed by the PalyonDofus project unless a particular component or third-party dependency specifies different licensing terms.

Third-party libraries, services, APIs, and other components remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.

## Disclaimer

This authorization does not grant ownership of PalyonDofus source code, trademarks, copyrighted assets, or other intellectual property.

The software is provided without any warranty regarding reverse-engineering activities. Researchers remain responsible for complying with applicable laws and third-party rights.
